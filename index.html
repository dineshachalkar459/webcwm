Our Sourcing Pipeline

The ChargeWave Lead Generation Engine

We don't just run ads. We execute an end-to-end qualification program that filters out tire-kickers and delivers ready-to-sign clients.

1
Outreach & Sourcing

Precise Database Hygiene

Our operation begins with an intern dedicating 5-10 hours weekly to scrap and deduplicate target databases. We ensure the contact points are 100% active, excluding international numbers or dead corporate emails.

2
Creative Production

Localized Problem-Based Ads

Harshada (our Creative Lead) develops custom, high-CTR static and video creatives based on installers' pain points. We target commercial land owners, petrol pumps, fleet operators, and high-net-worth homeowners.

3
Strict Pre-Qualification

3-Point Intent Screening

Inbound leads are manually checked. We verify: 1. Budget capacity (minimum threshold validation), 2. Land/Commercial site ownership, and 3. Implementation timeline. Only pre-qualified leads move forward.

4
Sales Scheduling

Automated Calendar Booking

Qualified prospects are automatically redirected to Akash Patale's scheduling pipeline. They select an appointment slot, input qualifying data, and are logged directly inside our HubSpot database.

5
Fulfillment & reporting

Weekly Pulse & Monthly Audits

Once a client is signed, we configure their Meta Business Manager pixels. Every Friday, they receive a qualified status breakdown. Every month, a Looker Studio funnel report and a Loom review are sent to keep deliverables transparent.
